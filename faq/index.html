Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about Mr. Bin trash bin washing, sanitizing, and deodorizing in Stafford & Fredericksburg, VA. $25 first bin, $20 each additional bin, per visit. No subscription, no contract.

How much does trash bin cleaning cost in Stafford, VA?

$25 for the first bin and $20 for each additional bin, per visit — a typical two-bin home is $45 total. There is no subscription and no contract; you pay only when we clean.

Do you require a subscription or a contract?

No. Mr. Bin is pay-per-visit: book a single cleaning whenever you want one. Some services charge less per visit only inside a recurring plan (one local plan's one-time clean is $55); our everyday pay-per-visit price for a first bin is $25.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Stafford, VA (22554) and Fredericksburg, VA (22401), including Aquia Harbour, Leeland Station, Embrey Mill, and Stafford Lakes.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Leave your bins at the curb after trash pickup and we'll wash, sanitize, and deodorize them there — most customers aren't home, and we text you a photo when it's done.

When do you clean — before or after trash pickup?

The day after your trash pickup, so the bins are empty. Clean day = trash day + 1.

What's included in a cleaning?

A full wash, sanitize, and deodorize of the inside and outside of each bin, done at your curb. We take the dirty water with us — nothing is left in your driveway or the street.

How often should I get my bins cleaned?

Most homes do well every 4–8 weeks; in Virginia summer heat, odor and flies build faster and many customers choose about every 4 weeks. Because there's no plan, you pick the timing — book again whenever the bins tell you it's time.

How do I book?

Book in under a minute at mrbinusa.com, or call/text (571) 589-5542. Tell us your address and how many bins; we'll confirm your clean day (the day after your trash pickup).

Can I give a bin cleaning as a gift — or use it for a home I'm selling?

Yes. Our $45 two-bin clean is a popular closing gift from realtors and a quick pre-listing fix before photos or an open house — email MrBinllc@gmail.com and we'll set it up.

Do you clean in winter?

Yes — we clean year-round. Some bin-cleaning services pause from December through February; Mr. Bin keeps running, and winter is a good time to reset bins before spring.
